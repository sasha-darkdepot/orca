1

Flat rows instead of cards

No borders, no card background — just simple rows with generous padding. Active item gets a very subtle highlight.

2

Hide search & group controls by default

Remove the always-visible search bar and All/PR Status/Repo toggle. Show them on demand (e.g. ⌘K or a filter icon).

3

Diff stats (+/- lines) on each row

Show lines added/removed right on the row instead of (or alongside) PR/CI metadata. Green/red coloring for active item.

4

Bigger text, more whitespace

Bump font sizes from 10-12px to 13-14px. More vertical padding between items. Less visual density overall.

5

Single-line items (name only)

Show just the worktree name on each row — hide branch, repo badge, PR, issue, comment by default. Reveal on hover or expand.

6

Minimal bottom toolbar

Just icons (help + settings), no "Add Repo" text button. Cleaner, more subtle.
